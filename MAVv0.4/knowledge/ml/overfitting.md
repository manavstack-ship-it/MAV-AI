# Overfitting

Overfitting occurs when a model memorizes training data.

An overfitted model performs poorly on unseen data.

Training accuracy becomes very high.

Testing accuracy becomes lower.

Complex models are more likely to overfit.

Small datasets increase overfitting risk.

Noise in data can also cause overfitting.

Regularization reduces overfitting.

Cross-validation improves model evaluation.

Early stopping prevents unnecessary training.

Dropout is commonly used in neural networks.

Feature selection reduces unnecessary complexity.

Collecting more data also helps.

The goal is to achieve good generalization.

A balanced model performs well on both training and testing data.S
